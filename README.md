# Hotel Booking & Cancellation Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes hotel booking data from 2015 to 2017 to understand booking cancellation patterns, hotel performance, customer behavior, and seasonal booking trends.

Using Microsoft Excel and Power Query, the dataset was cleaned, transformed, and analyzed to identify key business insights and potential opportunities to improve hotel booking management.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze overall hotel booking and cancellation rates.
- Compare cancellation patterns between City Hotels and Resort Hotels.
- Understand the relationship between booking lead time and cancellations.
- Identify cancellation trends across different market segments.
- Analyze monthly booking volumes.
- Compare cancellation behavior between first-time and repeated guests.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Excel
- Power Query
- Excel Formulas
- Pivot Tables / Summary Tables
- Charts & Dashboard

## 📂 Dataset Overview
- **Dataset:** Hotel Booking Demand
- **Total Records:** 119,390
- **Time Period:** 2015–2017
- **Cancelled Bookings:** 44,224
- **Overall Cancellation Rate:** 37.04%

## 🔍 Key Analysis & Findings

### 1. Hotel Type Comparison
- City Hotel cancellation rate: 41.73%
- Resort Hotel cancellation rate: 27.77%
- City Hotels had a 13.96 percentage-point higher cancellation rate.

### 2. Lead Time Analysis
- Bookings made 0–7 days in advance had a 9.63% cancellation rate.
- Bookings made 181+ days in advance had a 57.01% cancellation rate.
- Cancellation rates increased across the lead-time groups.

### 3. Market Segment Analysis
- Groups recorded the highest cancellation rate at 61.06%.
- Direct bookings had a cancellation rate of 15.34%.
- Corporate bookings had a cancellation rate of 18.73%.

### 4. Monthly Booking Trends
- August recorded the highest booking volume with 13,877 bookings.
- January recorded the lowest booking volume with 5,929 bookings.
- Monthly totals combine records from all three years.

### 5. Guest Behavior Analysis
- First-time guest cancellation rate: 37.79%.
- Repeated guest cancellation rate: 14.49%.
- Repeated-guest records showed a 23.30 percentage-point lower cancellation rate.

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Review group booking policies and cancellation conditions.
- Test reminder strategies for bookings made well in advance.
- Explore loyalty programs and repeat-guest retention initiatives.
- Investigate opportunities to improve direct bookings.
- Review hotel type, seasonality, and deposit conditions before making policy changes.

## ⚙️ Data Preparation
- Imported the dataset into Power Query.
- Checked column quality and data conversion errors.
- Replaced conversion errors in the children column with null values.
- Validated category totals against overall booking counts.
- Excluded two Undefined market-segment records from comparison charts while retaining them in reconciliation.

## ⚠️ Limitations
- The analysis is descriptive and does not establish causation.
- Monthly booking counts combine all three years.
- Average Daily Rate (ADR) does not represent total revenue or profit.
- The Undefined market segment contains only two records.
- Missing or invalid children values were not assumed to be zero.

## 📊 Project Outcome
The analysis identified important cancellation patterns across hotel types, booking lead times, market segments, and guest categories. These insights can help hotel management investigate cancellation risks and evaluate potential improvements in booking and customer retention strategies.
